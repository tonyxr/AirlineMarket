# Reduced 2022 ORD pipeline

The authoritative scope is `config/data/primary_ord_2022.json`. Its production recipe is `config/data/process_ord_2022_v1.json`; completed stage receipts and input/code hashes are under `results/scoped_processing/20261111_ord_v1`. These rules describe the executed loader, not a claim that the compiled empirical model or P3 calibration has passed.

## Geography, carrier attribution and accounting

1. Select O&D markets using Q2 DB1B only: UA/AA nonstops to/from ORD and single connections at ORD within the 16-airport FlightPrices pool. Retain all carrier and path alternatives within selected O&D markets, including UA/AA paths through other hubs. Q3 can never expand or fit this scope. A quote's quarter follows departure date, not search date.
2. Keep the marketing network and physical operator separately. The physical key is departure date, operator DOT ID, operator flight number, origin, destination and scheduled departure time. Consistent duplicate physical/network rows collapse; ambiguous shared marketing is explicit and its physical weight sums to one. Conflicting critical fields fail rather than silently select a row.
3. Filter T-100 to service class F and April–September 2022. Preserve operating-carrier route-month totals. Allocate those totals using observed **performed-flight** marketing shares on the same operator-route-month; cancelled flights do not dilute those weights. Keep unmatched capacity in `UNMAPPED`. These are estimated network allocations, not observed contract entitlements, aircraft-level seat counts or unsold inventory.
4. Use the ordered DB1B AirportGroup and ticket/operating carrier sequences. A same-network ticket sequence defines the network label; mixed paths remain MIXED. Validate every sequence against its endpoints and coupon count. Expand sampled passenger counts by 10. Keep bulk/nonpositive fare rows in traffic accounting but exclude them from fare initialization, with an explicit `fare_eligible` flag.
5. Retain both legs of a connection and endpoints outside ORD. FlightPrices offers with broken paths, endpoint mismatch, more than two legs, cross-date paths or unresolved operator attribution remain in the scoped panel with their eligibility/reason labels. They are not silently turned into core modeled products.
6. For regional quote codes, infer marketing identity only from a unique observed operator-route-departure-time match, or a unique operator-route-day match. FlightPrices lacks flight numbers. Ambiguity stays UNMAPPED; estimated seat allocation shares are never used to assign an individual offer's carrier.

## Calendar, prices and observation limits

FlightPrices collection starts April 16. A missing earlier search is unobserved, not zero demand. The scoped panel flags left truncation of the declared 60-day window. A complete 60-day Q2 departure window is available only from June 15. Connection construction must use airport time zones and scheduled elapsed minutes; subtracting two local HHMM clocks is invalid across time zones. No empirical compiler using the old local-clock shortcut is accepted as the real-data case.

Use quoted `totalFare` for all-in posted-fare moments; retain `baseFare` separately. BTS describes DB1B market fares as based on total ticket value including taxes and fees paid at purchase ([BTS Air Fares](https://data.bts.gov/stories/s/Air-Fares/gsyp-spgc), checked November 11, 2026). Paid fares and posted offers remain different sampling populations; equal tax basis does not identify a purchase/choice process. Simulator cash currently records its charged fare. Empirical airline profit needs an explicit tax/pass-through treatment before being interpreted as net carrier revenue.

Reported seatsRemaining is an offer display, not independently observed residual physical inventory. Its cap and relationship to booking classes must be fitted and validated; the panel does not identify hidden inventory ground truth. Daily NOAA observations are available for all 183 April–September days. Seven attempted hourly files were HTML error content and are excluded.

## Executed V1 input checks and unresolved differences

The input allocation conserves all 11,654,918 seats, 10,240,261 passengers and 75,392 T-100 performed departures. Maximum per-source seat discrepancy is 1.82e-12 from floating allocation. 8,618 seats (0.07394%) remain UNMAPPED. DB1B expansion errors are zero in each quarter. These checks are input accounting; they are not compiled-model reconciliation.

Marketing On-Time contains 77,463 scheduled and 75,245 performed physical flights in scope. T-100 contains 75,392 performed **segments**. Before diagnostic reconstruction, 296 operator-route-month cells differ and 110/736 differ by more than 2%. The operating-only Reporting On-Time files contain only 77,210 scoped scheduled flights: 253 marketing flights are absent. The omitted operators are identified in the diagnostic output. The primary schedule therefore remains the marketing table.

The independent diagnostic `pipeline.reconcile_departures` uses already downloaded reporting diversion airports and destination-reached flags to reconstruct physical hops; cancelled-before-flight records contribute no hops. Air returns and intermediate diversions are kept as physical paths, excluding zero-length airport pairs. Hops are attributed to scheduled departure month, so actual month rollovers can remain different. BTS distinguishes scheduled destination from diversion landings ([On-Time technical reporting directive](https://www.bts.gov/explore-topics-and-geography/modes/aviation/number-40-technical-directive-reporting-time)); this is a reason to test the definitions, not evidence that a particular residual is explained.

`analysis/validation/departure_coverage.py` applies only the observed reporting diversion correction to the marketing totals. Nonreporting regional operators retain their original marketing counts; their unknown corrections are not imputed. Every remaining operator-route-month discrepancy is exported, including ad-hoc/foreign-carrier records with no marketing observation. No residual is labeled explained merely because its magnitude is small. V1 remains incomplete until actual compiled seats, departures, O&D flows and schedule counts are reconciled, or each remaining mismatch has a documented source-specific rule. No tolerance has been relaxed.

## Next empirical stage

The audited intermediate calendar is now built by `pipeline.dated_network` using `config/data/build_ord_dated_network_v1.json`. Evidence/tables are under `results/V1/20261111_dated_network_v1/attempt-0001`: 183 cohorts, 59,860 legs and 374,725 products (314,865 connections). Resource IDs retain physical identity and both operator/marketer. Arrivals are departure UTC plus scheduled elapsed minutes, checked against the destination clock; 2400 rolls to the next local day. Connections stay within one marketing network and actual local departure cohort with 40–300-minute waits. All 7,856 performed partner legs with usable capacity inputs remain.

This calendar describes observed service: it excludes 1,798 observed cancellations explicitly, plus four UA/YX performed flights lacking positive matching T-100 performed departures. It is not a forecast of future cancellations. Seats per leg are operator-route-month SEATS/DEPARTURES_PERFORMED; they are **not** rescaled by On-Time counts to force agreement. The proposed two-point private-capacity prior preserves this mean; its 10% spread is an assumption. At the marketed leg-month level, 197/534 seat/departure comparisons remain discrepant. Converting these tables to a full behavioral MarketSpec and reconciling its remaining counts is still required. Q3 structure is prepared with the same rules; Q3 traffic/fares have not fitted parameters.

Build dated cohorts from these marketing schedules and operator-based capacity estimates; record capacity rounding/distribution assumptions and excluded records. Construct Q2 target moments and fit the full P3 simulated-moment ensemble on calibration seeds 400000+. Preserve the nonpurchase, taste and arrival identification limits. Freeze fitted parameters before Q3 V5 evaluation; do not use Q3 to tune scope, moments or parameters. Primary ORD is supported by the reduced data; secondary hub and historical Paper C expansions are deferred under the owner's scope decision.


## Behavioral initialization and resource preflight

`pipeline.behavioral_network` and `config/data/initialize_ord_behavioral_v1.json` translate the audited dated core tables into an **uncalibrated** behavioral specification. The two strategic networks remain UA/AA. An additional OUTSIDE carrier/product/resource per O&D is a fixed aggregate ticket alternative, not a measured airline or physical flight. It covers all nonrepresented ticket paths, including off-network core-carrier paths; nonpurchase is a separate utility-zero option. Synthetic proxy resources are excluded from physical-resource reconciliation. Their large finite capacity is an explicit technical approximation, checked against realized request counts in the resource probe.

Q2 target shares and fares initialize reference utilities and prices. Arrival conversion, uniform booking profile, segment preferences/sensitivities and operating costs are assumptions. Initial prices use weighted medians of target-bin medians; missing cells use a declared pooled Q2 prior recorded in metadata. No increasing-fare sign is imposed. This initializer does not implement exposure-matched moment fitting, a weather/cancellation forecast, or the P3 CEM ensemble. Q3 parameters have not been fitted or evaluated.

`sim.revenue_proxy` fits a nonnegative affine Q2 totalFare-minus-baseFare channel using streaming sufficient statistics. Invalid source pairs remain counted as exclusions. This is a base/all-in **proxy**, not verified taxes or retained airline revenue. Its analyst-only contribution calculation leaves native all-in cash/choice untouched. Native planners do not optimize this proxy; empirical net-profit optimization still needs a compatible objective integration. Full original-fare refunds are the only supported sidecar accounting convention.

`sim.market_local_tape` stores only each potential Poisson request's market taste vector. All other named-stream draws retain their exact values; taste layout changes but remains iid and policy independent. Dense projections of the same local tastes reproduce paths and snapshot restoration. It is used only in separately declared resource studies, not silently substituted into ongoing campaigns or unverified filtering/batch paths.
